    How to read the result
    • The percentage is the model's calibrated estimate that the face was manipulated. It is not proof either way.
    • Three bands instead of a verdict: no manipulation detected, uncertain, likely manipulated. The cut-offs were set on validation data, and the uncertain band never shrinks to nothing.
    • A low score means "not detected by this model", never "real". New face-swap tools, heavy compression and very small faces all lower accuracy.
    • The heatmap shows where the model looked on the most suspicious frame. It is a hint for a human reviewer, not evidence.
    • Only the largest face is scored. Frames without a detectable face are skipped.
