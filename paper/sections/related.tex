\section{Related work}

\paragraph{Benchmarks.} FaceForensics++~\citep{rossler2019faceforensics} provides 1{,}000 real
YouTube videos and four manipulation methods at several compression levels, and is the most
common training set. Celeb-DF~\citep{li2020celebdf} offers higher-quality face swaps with fewer
visible artifacts. The DeepFake Detection Challenge dataset~\citep{dolhansky2020dfdc} is large and
diverse, DeeperForensics-1.0~\citep{jiang2020deeperforensics} adds real-world perturbations, and
WildDeepfake~\citep{zi2020wilddeepfake} collects fakes from the internet. DF40~\citep{yan2024df40}
covers about forty newer methods. DeepfakeBench~\citep{yan2023deepfakebench} standardizes
cross-dataset evaluation and documents the generalization gap we target.

\paragraph{Detectors.} CNN classifiers such as Xception~\citep{chollet2017xception} and
EfficientNet~\citep{tan2019efficientnet} are strong same-dataset baselines. Face
X-ray~\citep{li2020facexray} and self-blended images (SBI)~\citep{shiohara2022sbi} instead train
on synthetic blends of real faces, so the model learns the blending boundary common to most face
swaps. Vision Transformers~\citep{dosovitskiy2021vit} and large pretrained encoders such as
CLIP~\citep{radford2021clip} and DINOv2~\citep{oquab2024dinov2} have been shown to generalize
better to unseen generators when adapted lightly~\citep{ojha2023towards}. Parameter-efficient
fine-tuning with LoRA~\citep{hu2022lora} keeps most pretrained weights frozen, which limits
overfitting to the training generators.

\paragraph{Calibration, explanation and fairness.} Modern networks are often over-confident;
temperature scaling~\citep{guo2017calibration} is a simple, effective fix that leaves the ranking
unchanged. Attention rollout~\citep{abnar2020rollout} and Grad-CAM~\citep{selvaraju2017gradcam}
show where a model looks. Deepfake detectors have been found to perform unevenly across
demographic groups~\citep{trinh2021examination,xu2022comprehensive}, which matters most for false
alarms on real people's videos.
