\begin{abstract}
Face-manipulation detectors routinely reach near-perfect AUC on the dataset they were trained on,
then lose much of that accuracy on videos made with other methods or collected elsewhere, which
is the situation every deployed detector faces. We study how far a parameter-efficient Vision
Transformer can close this gap when trained on FaceForensics++ alone. Our detector fine-tunes a
pretrained ViT-B/16 with low-rank adapters on face crops, adds self-blended training images that
teach blending artifacts rather than one generator's style, and uses heavy degradation
augmentation. Scores are calibrated with temperature scaling and reported as three bands
instead of a verdict. Trained only on FaceForensics++, the model reaches a video-level AUC of
\res{X.XX $\pm$ X.XX} on Celeb-DF v2 and \res{X.XX} on unseen manipulation methods, against
\res{X.XX} for the strongest baseline, and fits on a single 8\,GB laptop GPU. We report
robustness to common degradations, calibration, results per demographic group, failure cases,
and ablations of every design choice. Code and weights are released for research use.
\end{abstract}
